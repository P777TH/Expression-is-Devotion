Startup work package v1 - draft for review

The Devotion Engine

Art becomes funding. Funding becomes milestones. Milestones become testimonies. Testimonies bring the next artists and supporters. A lean, self-funding way to launch Expression is Devotion, without losing a single part of the original vision.

The essence we protect

Expression is devotion

Art is offered as worship and service. The artist's "why" sits next to every piece.

Vetted missions only

Vetting comes before visibility. Only approved missions receive funds.

Staged, evidenced release

Tranches tied to milestones and testimonies, approved by two people.

Testimony is the heartbeat

Every euro has a story trail back to the supporter.

Trust by design

Separation of duties, audit log, GDPR consent, no card data stored.

Human authority

The board decides about money. AI assists, never approves.

The flywheel

Artists donate art→ Campaign Drop→ Supporters bid, buy prints, fund a milestone→ Stichting grants tranche→ Mission delivers + testimony→ Shared with churches, social, newsletter↻

One product: the Campaign Drop

1 to 3 missions

Vetted, with a public goal split into 2 to 4 milestones.

10 to 25 artworks

Donated by Founding Artists, curated for readiness.

7 to 10 days

Timed online auction, soft close, optional live closing evening in a church.

4 ways to give

Bid, buy a print, fund a milestone, join the Devotion Circle.

Four drops a year: Lent/Easter, Pentecost, Harvest, Advent, plus 72-hour micro-drops in between.

Funding engine: two streams, never mixed

Mission fund

Auctions, prints, fund-a-milestone, earmarked gifts. 100% granted to missions (minus provider fees, shown openly).

Operations fund

Devotion Circle share, optional tips, drop sponsors, foundation grants. About 40 to 60 Circle members cover Stage 0 costs.

Grant model

The stichting receives income and grants it in tranches under a grant agreement, with a dual-authorisation bank account. To confirm with a legal advisor.

Three stages, one data model

Stage 0 Launch

Thin Next.js site + Supabase EU + Mollie or Stripe + Brevo or MailerLite + Tally + Canva frames. About 50 to 120 euro a month.

Stage 1 Platform v1

Role portals, in-app approvals, own auction engine, post formats. Built from the ERDs on the same codebase.

Stage 2 Scale

API posting, shipping tables, dashboards, more languages. Only when metrics justify it.
