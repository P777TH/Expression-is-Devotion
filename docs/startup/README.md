## Why this package exists

The BRD and the four ERDs describe the **full destination**: a modular platform
with auctions, tranches, testimonies, newsletter, social posting and a governed
admin portal. That is the right long-term shape, but building all of it before
the first euro reaches a mission is slow, costly, and risky.

This package turns the same vision into a **startup path**: launch small, fund
missions within weeks, learn from real supporters, and grow into the ERD design
one module at a time. Nothing in the BRD is thrown away. Everything is sequenced.

## The idea in one paragraph

**The Devotion Engine** is a campaign-based funding engine. Four times a year
(plus small "micro-drops" in between), Expression is Devotion runs a **Campaign
Drop** for one to three vetted missions. Artists donate pieces to the drop,
supporters bid, buy prints, or "fund a milestone" directly, and a monthly
**Devotion Circle** of recurring givers keeps the lights on. The stichting
receives the funds as its own charitable income and **grants** them to each
mission in milestone tranches, with every release evidenced by a testimony
that flows back to supporters, churches and social channels. Each testimony
attracts the next artists and supporters, so the engine compounds.

## Startup principles used

1. **Campaigns before marketplace.** Concentrated, time-boxed drops create
   urgency, stories and press, and keep volunteer effort predictable.
2. **Assemble first, build what is unique.** Use proven EU tools for payments,
   email, forms and scheduling. Build only the devotion-specific parts:
   mission-to-art linking, milestone tranches, testimony trail.
3. **Own the data model from day one.** Day-one tools mirror the ERD entities
   and IDs so that moving to the custom platform is a migration, not a rebuild.
4. **Grant model, not custody model.** The stichting receives gifts and sale
   proceeds as its own income and grants them out in tranches. This is the
   usual way Dutch charities work and avoids "holding third-party money"
   (to confirm with a legal advisor, see decision D1).
5. **100% to mission, transparently.** Mission proceeds are never used for
   running costs. Operations are funded separately (Devotion Circle share,
   optional donor tips, grants).
6. **Low touch by default.** Every recurring task has a template, a checklist,
   and a named owner. If a task happens more than three times, automate it.
7. **Measure what matters.** One North Star: *milestones funded and evidenced
   per quarter.*
