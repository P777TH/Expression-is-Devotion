---
title: Assemble-First Platform Option
description: Capability-level use of existing products instead of building every platform feature
---

## Summary

Use existing products for selected capabilities such as identity, CMS,
newsletter, auction storefront, payment onboarding, media storage, or social
publishing. Build only domain workflows and governance that products cannot
meet. No single assembled platform is assumed to cover the full scope.

## Potential Value

* Reduce custom development and operations for commodity functions.
* Test demand, onboarding, newsletter, or public-content workflows sooner.
* Use managed security and delivery capabilities where controls and contracts
  fit the organization.

## Risks to Validate

* Does the product support eligible international payouts without the
  foundation unlawfully holding funds?
* Can approval separation, audit export, erasure, retention, consent, and
  cross-product identity be implemented consistently?
* Can data and financial evidence be exported and migrated?
* Are EU residency, subprocessors, availability, support, fees, and exit costs
  acceptable?
* Do auction and newsletter terms align with the legal treatment of sales and
  charitable gifts?

## Decision Gate

Compare products against written scenarios and contract review. Prefer
integrations that keep canonical records exportable. Do not select a product
based only on a demo or subscription price. Legal/provider feasibility for
funds remains a prerequisite.
